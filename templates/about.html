Our Story

PhishGuard was founded in 2025 with a simple yet powerful mission: to protect internet users from the growing threat of phishing attacks. As cyber threats continue to evolve and become more sophisticated, we recognized the need for an accessible, user-friendly solution that leverages advanced technology to keep people safe online.

Our Mission

To protect internet users from phishing attacks through innovative technology, education, and awareness. We strive to make the internet a safer place by providing accessible tools that empower individuals and organizations to identify and avoid phishing threats.

We believe that everyone deserves to navigate the digital world without fear of having their personal information compromised. By combining cutting-edge machine learning algorithms with user-friendly interfaces, we're making advanced security accessible to all.

Our Vision

A world where phishing attacks are no longer effective because users are equipped with the knowledge and tools to recognize and avoid them. We envision a future where cybersecurity education is universal, and protective technology is seamlessly integrated into everyday online activities.

We're working toward a digital ecosystem where security doesn't come at the expense of convenience, and where even the most vulnerable internet users are protected from sophisticated phishing attempts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about PhishGuard

How accurate is PhishGuard's phishing detection?

PhishGuard's detection system has an accuracy rate of 98.7%, which is among the highest in the industry. Our machine learning algorithms are continuously trained on new data to improve detection capabilities and adapt to emerging phishing techniques.

Is PhishGuard free to use?

Yes, PhishGuard offers a free basic plan that includes URL scanning and basic reporting features. We also offer premium plans for individuals and organizations that need advanced features, detailed analytics, and API access.

How does PhishGuard protect my privacy?

We take privacy seriously. PhishGuard only collects the data necessary to provide our services and improve our detection algorithms. We never sell user data to third parties, and all scanned URLs are processed securely. You can read our full privacy policy for more details.

Can I integrate PhishGuard with my organization's security systems?

Yes, PhishGuard offers API access for enterprise customers, allowing seamless integration with existing security infrastructure. Our team can provide technical support and documentation to help with the integration process.

How can I report a phishing site that wasn't detected?

If you encounter a phishing site that wasn't detected by our system, please report it through the "Report Phishing" feature in your dashboard or contact our support team. Your reports help us improve our detection algorithms and protect other users.
